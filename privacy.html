Privacy Policy — நல்நாள் · NalNaal

Last updated: July 26, 2026

நல்நாள் (NalNaal) is a Tamil daily-calendar app. We believe your privacy matters, and we have built this app to collect as little as possible — in fact, nothing.

Information we collect

None. NalNaal does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The calendar data is bundled with the app and loaded over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

Location

NalNaal never requests or stores your location. On certain days, the app offers a "find a nearby temple" button. Tapping it opens Google Maps with a temple search. At that point, Google Maps — not NalNaal — may use your device location to show nearby results, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. NalNaal itself never sees or keeps your location.

Third-party services

The app opens Google Maps only when you choose to search for a temple. No other third-party services are used.

Data sharing

Because we collect no data, we have nothing to share, sell, or disclose.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
