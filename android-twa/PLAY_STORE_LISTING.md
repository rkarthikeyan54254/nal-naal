## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
நல்நாள் (NalNaal) is a beautiful Tamil daily calendar built for families who follow the traditional Pambu Panchangam. Open it each morning to see the sacred character of today — the presiding deity, nakshatram, tithi, and any festival or observance — all in one glance.

🪔 A DEITY FOR EVERY DAY
Every one of the 27 nakshatrams is mapped to its presiding deity, so each day greets you with a real image of the god who governs that star — from Murugan and Perumal to Amman, Ganesha, and more.

📿 ROOTED IN THE PAMBU PANCHANGAM
The calendar is computed with precise astronomy (Swiss Ephemeris) and then anchored to the traditional Vakyam Pambu Panchangam, so the auspicious days align with the panchangam your family trusts.

🎉 EVERY MAJOR FESTIVAL
Deepavali, Pongal, Karthigai Deepam, Krishna Jayanthi, Vinayaka Chaturthi, Navaratri, Thaipusam, Maha Shivaratri, Tamil New Year and many more — each with the right date, deity, and a short note on its meaning.

🌙 DAILY OBSERVANCES
Ekadasi, Pradosham, Sashti, Pournami, Amavasai, Aadi/Thai Velli, and other recurring vratams are marked automatically, so you never miss a fasting or worship day.

📍 FIND A TEMPLE NEARBY
On observance days, tap to find the nearest relevant temple — a Shiva temple on Pradosham, a Perumal temple on Ekadasi, an Amman temple on Velli days. It opens Google Maps to guide you. Your location stays private — the app never stores it.

🗓️ BROWSE ANY DAY
Swipe or tap through the year, jump to any date, and see the full panchangam for that day. A clean "Coming Up" list shows the next festivals at a glance.

✨ MADE WITH CARE
A calm, reverent design in temple gold and kumkum red, with authentic Tamil typography. No ads, no clutter — just today's blessing, one darshan at a time.

நல்நாள் வாழ்த்துக்கள். May every day be auspicious.
```
